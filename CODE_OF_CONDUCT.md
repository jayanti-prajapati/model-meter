# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in Model Meter welcoming and respectful to people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential data or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately sharing credentials or customer data.
- Trolling or sustained disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy. To report an issue, contact the repository maintainers privately through the channels configured in the GitHub repository.
